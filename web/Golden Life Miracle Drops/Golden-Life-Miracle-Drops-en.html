Hyaluronic acid

Hyaluronic acid
A substance that occurs naturally and is produced in our bodies. Its main function is to retain water, hydrating tissues and maintaining sufficient moisture in them. It helps nourish the skin, making it healthier and more supple. It thus limits wrinkle formation and aids regeneration and overall skin rejuvenation. It effectively helps with wound healing and joint pain, improving joint mobility. Regular use of hyaluronic acid delays the onset of wrinkles.

Use and safety

Directions for use:

Apply in the morning to cleansed skin on the face and neck (3 - 5 drops). Massage in gently. Wait a few minutes before applying make-up. For even better absorption, we recommend applying to damp skin.

volume 50 ml

Storage:

Store at temperatures of 5-25 °C and protect the product from sunlight.

Restrictions:

For external use. Keep out of reach of children. Use by the date stated on the packaging.
