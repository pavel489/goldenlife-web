Nicotinamide riboside (NR)

Spermidine

Spermidine is a naturally occurring polyamine found in wheat germ extract (Triticum vulgare). It is one of the modern longevity ingredients studied in the field of cell regeneration and healthy ageing. The daily dose (2 capsules) contains 250 mg of extract.

×

Resveratrol

Resveratrol from Japanese knotweed extract (Polygonum cuspidatum) is one of the well-known antioxidants associated with cell protection against oxidative stress and support for healthy ageing. The daily dose (2 capsules) contains 300 mg of extract, equivalent to 150 mg of pure resveratrol.

×

Hyaluronic acid

Hyaluronic acid occurs naturally in the skin and connective tissues, where it helps bind water and supports hydration. Its natural production declines with age, which is why it is a popular ingredient in beauty supplements. The daily dose (2 capsules) contains 300 mg of hyaluronic acid.

×

Phytoceramides

Phytoceramides from wheat germ extract are plant analogues of ceramides, which are a natural part of the skin barrier. Together with hyaluronic acid, they support skin hydration and comfort. The daily dose (2 capsules) contains 80 mg of phytoceramides.

×

Astaxanthin

Astaxanthin is a natural carotenoid produced by microalgae and is one of the powerful natural antioxidants. The daily dose (2 capsules) contains 20 mg of astaxanthin.

×

Quercetin (Sophora japonica)

Quercetin is a flavonoid that occurs naturally in many plants. In this product, it comes from Sophora japonica extract with a high quercetin content. The daily dose (2 capsules) contains 150 mg of extract.

×

Apigenin

Apigenin is a natural flavonoid obtained from chamomile extract and is used in modern longevity formulas focused on the body's overall balance. The daily dose (2 capsules) contains 150 mg of chamomile extract.

×

Alpha-lipoic acid

Alpha-lipoic acid is an antioxidant that acts in both aqueous and fatty environments and is naturally involved in energy metabolism. The daily dose (2 capsules) contains 100 mg of alpha-lipoic acid.
