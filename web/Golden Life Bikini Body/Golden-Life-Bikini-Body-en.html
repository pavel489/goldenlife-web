Directions for use:

Recommended daily dose: 2 capsules. Ideally take 45 minutes before a meal or at least 2 hours after a meal. Take the capsules with plenty of water and swallow them whole – do not chew or break them. Suitable for long-term use.

Storage:

Store at room temperature (20–25 °C), in a dry, dark place away from direct sunlight. Protect from frost and moisture. Keep out of reach of children.

Warnings:

This product is a food supplement – it is not a substitute for a varied and balanced diet. Not intended for children or pregnant or breastfeeding women. Not suitable when taking blood-thinning medication (anticoagulants). Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.
