How to use

Instructions for use:

The recommended daily dose is one teaspoon (2 g). Pour over hot water (not boiling) and allow to steep for 5-10 minutes. Drink 1-2 cups daily, ideally in the morning and evening.

Storage:

Store in a dry place away from sunlight, at a temperature of up to 25 °C. After opening, store in a sealed container and use within 6 months.

Restrictions:

Food supplement. Not a substitute for a varied diet. Do not exceed the recommended daily dosage. The tea blend contains herbs that may affect blood clotting, so the product is not suitable for pregnant or breastfeeding women or people with blood clotting problems. Keep out of reach of children.
