Directions for use:

Recommended daily dose: 2 capsules. Take after a meal or before exercise in the morning. During the first week, due to the synephrine content, we recommend starting with 1 capsule daily. Swallow the capsules whole, without chewing. Increase fluid intake during the day. Do not take within 6 hours before bedtime, as it may interfere with falling asleep.

Storage:

Store in a dry place, out of reach of children and away from sunlight.

Warnings:

Contains caffeine. Not suitable for children, during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or in case of allergy to any of the ingredients used. Not a substitute for a varied diet. Due to its forskolin content, this product should not be used by people with thyroid disease, blood-clotting disorders, cardiovascular diseases, asthma or stomach ulcers.

Ingredients

Coleus forskohlii, acetyl-L-carnitine, taurine, stinging nettle, green tea extract, dandelion, piperine, hesperidin, synephrine, vegetable capsule (HPMC). Contains naturally occurring caffeine.
