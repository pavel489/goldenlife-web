How it works

Vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid) is a powerful antioxidant. It supports normal immune system function, contributes to the protection of cells from oxidative stress and to collagen formation for healthy bones, skin and blood vessels.

Effects

  • Reduces physical and mental tiredness
  • Aids collagen formation for normal bone function
  • Supports iron absorption
  • Protects against oxidative stress
