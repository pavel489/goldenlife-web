Nicotinamide riboside (NAD+ precursor)

Nicotinamide riboside is a modern NAD+ precursor that supports cellular energy, regeneration and processes associated with longevity. NAD+ is a coenzyme important for cellular energy metabolism, and its levels naturally decline with age.

Supplementing an NAD+ precursor helps support vitality, the body's regeneration and natural cell renewal. Beauty & Longevity NAD+ thus supports not only appearance, but also energy and the body's functioning from within.

Peptan® marine collagen

This collagen beauty drink is based on hydrolysed Peptan® fish collagen, one of the most absorbable forms of marine collagen. The collagen peptides are pre-broken down to an optimal size, so they are absorbed efficiently and reach where they are needed — the skin, joints and connective tissues.

Regular collagen supplementation supports skin elasticity, firmness and hydration, helps reduce fine lines and also supports hair, nails and the musculoskeletal system.

×

Hyaluronic acid

Hyaluronic acid is a natural component of the skin and helps bind water in tissues. It therefore supports deep hydration, elasticity and a fresh skin appearance.

Together with marine collagen, it creates a powerful combination to support a youthful skin appearance and reduce the feeling of dry or tired skin.

×

Resveratrol, coenzyme Q10 and alpha-lipoic acid

The three powerful antioxidants support cell protection against oxidative stress as well as the vitality of the body and skin.

Resveratrol from Japanese knotweed extract is known for its anti-ageing effects and support for healthy ageing. Coenzyme Q10 supports cellular energy and a youthful skin appearance. Alpha-lipoic acid acts as a universal antioxidant in both aqueous and fatty environments, complementing comprehensive cell protection.

MSM

MSM is an organic form of sulphur that is important for the formation of collagen and keratin — the basic building blocks of hair, nails and skin.

It supports joint flexibility and recovery after physical exertion and, together with collagen and hyaluronic acid, forms an important part of comprehensive care for beauty and the musculoskeletal system.

CocoMineral® coconut water

CocoMineral® coconut water concentrate provides the body with natural electrolytes such as potassium, sodium, magnesium and calcium. These support optimal hydration, mineral balance and proper cell function.

Hydration plays an important role not only in energy and vitality, but also in a fresh skin appearance and the body's overall comfort throughout the day.
