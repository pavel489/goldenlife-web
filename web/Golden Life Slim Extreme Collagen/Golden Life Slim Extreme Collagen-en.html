Garcinia cambogia

A tropical plant native to Asia that has been used for centuries to promote a feeling of fullness. Its key active ingredient is hydroxycitric acid (HCA), which improves digestion, reduces appetite and also supports fat burning.

×

Hyaluronic acid

A substance that occurs naturally and is produced in our bodies. Its main function is to retain water, hydrating tissues and maintaining sufficient moisture within them. It helps nourish the skin, making it healthier and more elastic. In this way, it limits wrinkle formation and helps the skin regenerate and rejuvenate overall. It effectively helps with wound healing and joint pain, improving joint mobility.

×

Minerals and enzymes

Bromelain and papain are a unique combination of two enzymes found in pineapple and papaya. Both have a positive effect on digestion and influence protein breakdown, which is particularly appreciated by athletes. The zinc in the formula plays a role in cell division, contributes to normal metabolism and protects cells from free radicals.

×

B complex

B vitamins are essential for normal metabolism and nervous system function. They also enable the absorption of substances and help the body make better use of them. Vitamins B2, B3 and B5 contribute to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue, while vitamins B2 and B3 maintain normal skin and mucous membranes.

×

Vitamins C and E

Vitamin C is important for the formation of new collagen, has a positive effect on the immune system, and contributes to the protection and formation of new cells, better recovery and reduced tiredness. Vitamin E is a very powerful antioxidant, is important for a number of oxidation-reduction processes and stabilises certain hormones.

×

Green tea

It contains antioxidants and helps fight free radicals. It supports healthy vision and reduces the risk of heart disease. The effects of green tea enable calorie burning, contribute to dopamine production and support productivity.

Directions for use:

Mix 1 scoop (8 g) into 200–300 ml of water. Take 1× daily, preferably in the morning or before noon. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.

Storage:

Store in a dry and cool place, away from direct sunlight, at a temperature of up to 25 °C. Keep out of reach of children.

Warnings:

Food supplement. Not suitable for pregnant or breastfeeding women or people with an allergy to any of the ingredients. Not a substitute for a varied and balanced diet. This product contains caffeine – use in the afternoon or evening is not recommended.
