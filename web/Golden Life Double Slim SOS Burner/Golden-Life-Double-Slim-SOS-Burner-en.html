Chitosan

Chitosan is a natural fibre obtained from crustaceans that acts as an effective ‘fat trap’. It binds fats and cholesterol compounds in the digestive tract and prevents their absorption. This helps reduce calorie intake, support weight loss and maintain healthy cholesterol levels. Its unique ability to form a gel in the stomach also prolongs the feeling of fullness and improves control over your diet.

Contains chitosan from crustaceans. People allergic to crustaceans should not take this product.

How it works

Slim SOS Fat Burner uses the synergy of five active ingredients to support weight loss effectively. Its foundation is chitosan – a natural fibre that binds fats in the digestive tract and prevents their absorption, helping to reduce calorie intake. White bean extract slows the breakdown of carbohydrates and maintains stable blood sugar levels, limiting sharp energy fluctuations and cravings for sweets. Beetroot, rich in antioxidants, supports vitality, tissue oxygenation and detoxification of the body. Apple cider vinegar naturally supports digestion, the production of digestive juices and fat metabolism, while chromium, a key trace element, stabilises blood glucose levels and reduces cravings for sweets. Thanks to this comprehensive combination, the product acts on several levels – blocking the absorption of excess calories, supporting healthy digestion, providing energy and helping to maintain control over body weight without drastic diets.
