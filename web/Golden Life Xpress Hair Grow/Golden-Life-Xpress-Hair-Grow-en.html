How it works

Xpress Hair Grow nourishes hair, nails and skin from within. At its core is biotin 5 000 mcg together with methionine and cysteine — the building blocks of keratin, the main protein in hair and nails. Silicon from field horsetail supports the strength and elasticity of the hair fibre.

Astaxanthin and vitamins C and E protect cells from oxidative stress, zinc and copper are involved in collagen and keratin formation, and the B-complex contributes to reduced tiredness and skin health. Thanks to this synergy, the product reduces hair breakage and hair loss, accelerates hair growth and supports the healthy appearance of nails, eyelashes and eyebrows.
