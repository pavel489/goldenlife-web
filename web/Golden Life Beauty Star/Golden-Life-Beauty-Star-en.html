Biotin (vitamin B7)

Biotin (Vitamin B7)

Biotin is a key vitamin for healthy hair, skin and nails. It supports the formation of keratin, the main component of hair, increasing its density and strength. It also helps reduce hair breakage and improves skin elasticity. Biotin deficiency can lead to dry skin, brittle hair and weakened nails, so a regular intake is essential for maintaining a healthy appearance and vitality.
