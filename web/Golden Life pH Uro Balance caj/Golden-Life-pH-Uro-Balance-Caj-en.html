How to use

Directions for use:

The recommended daily dose is one teaspoon (2 g). Pour over hot water (not boiling) and steep for 5-10 minutes. Drink 1-2 cups daily, ideally in the morning and evening.

Storage:

Store in a dry place away from sunlight at temperatures up to 25 °C. After opening, store in a closed container and use within 6 months.

Restrictions:

Food supplement. Not a substitute for a varied diet. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose. Pregnant and breastfeeding women and people with kidney disease should avoid this tea because of its effects on the urinary tract and kidneys. If you have health problems, we recommend consulting a doctor. Keep out of reach of children.
