Aquamin® Soluble

A premium marine complex obtained from red seaweed, containing more than 72 minerals and trace elements in a naturally bioavailable form. This means the body can actually use them – unlike conventional mineral supplements. Aquamin® supports proper muscle, nervous system and bone function, helps maintain vitality throughout the day and also hydrates the body at a cellular level. Thanks to its unique composition, it provides energy, reduces fatigue and is also ideal during weight-loss diets or after exercise, when the body loses important minerals.

CAROLEAN™

A patented combination of carob extract and prickly pear that acts as a natural appetite regulator. CAROLEAN™ forms a gentle gel in the stomach, inducing a longer-lasting feeling of fullness and satiety. It also slows the absorption of fats and sugars, helping stabilise energy levels and supporting weight control. The result? Fewer sugar cravings, better control over your diet and an easier path to a slimmer figure – without drastic diets.

LactoSpore® probiotics

One of the most clinically validated probiotic cultures – Bacillus coagulans MTCC 5856 – which is resistant to stomach acid and acts exactly where it is needed: in the intestines. Each serving contains 8,9 billion CFU that restore the balance of the gut microbiota, support healthy digestion and improve nutrient absorption from food. A strong, healthy gut = faster metabolism, stronger immunity and less heaviness after meals. Probiotics also have a positive effect on mood and overall wellbeing, something you will particularly appreciate during weight loss.

OPTI7DIGEST® enzyme blend

A unique complex of seven digestive enzymes (amylase, lactase, protease, lipase, cellulase, bromelain and papain) that helps the body process proteins, fats and carbohydrates more efficiently. This reduces bloating and heaviness after meals and allows the body to make better use of nutrients for energy and recovery. Bromelain from pineapple and papain from papaya also contribute to natural fat burning and faster muscle recovery after physical activity. OPTI7DIGEST® therefore supports not only weight loss but also overall digestive comfort and vitality.

Prebiotic FOS fibre

Natural fibre that serves as food for the “good” bacteria in the intestines. It supports the growth of healthy gut microbiota, improves digestion and helps absorb key nutrients. This contributes to a feeling of lightness, better immunity and healthy metabolism.

Multivitamin premix

Contains 12 bioactive vitamins in state-of-the-art forms – including active derivatives (riboflavin-5-phosphate, pyridoxal-5-phosphate, L-5-methylfolate, methylcobalamin and others). These vitamins support energy metabolism, proper nervous system function, immunity and healthy skin. The comprehensive blend also helps the body cope better with stress and fatigue during a weight-loss diet.

Chromium (picolinate)

A trace element essential for proper sugar and fat metabolism. It contributes to maintaining normal blood glucose levels, helping limit fluctuations in energy and preventing sugar cravings. This supports better hunger control and maintenance of stable body weight.

MCT oil powder

A source of so-called “quick fats” that are readily converted into energy in the body rather than stored. MCT oil supports metabolism, provides energy during physical and mental exertion and helps prolong satiety. It is an ideal supplement for weight loss and an active lifestyle.

Directions for use:

Mix 1 scoop (30 g) into 200–250 ml of water or milk. Take 1× daily, ideally after training or as a snack replacement.

Storage:

Store in a cool, dry place at temperatures up to 25 °C, out of reach of children and away from direct sunlight. Close the packaging carefully after opening.

Warnings:

Food supplement. Not a substitute for a varied diet. Not suitable for children or pregnant or breastfeeding women. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose. Caution is advised for people with an allergy to milk or soya.
